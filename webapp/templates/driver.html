Iris-based relative eye direction uses MediaPipe Face Mesh landmarks but is not calibrated to a particular vehicle or road direction. Eyeglasses, light, pose, and camera placement can produce false alarms. Optional phone-object detection only indicates a visible phone; it cannot determine whether the driver is using it. It may miss small/occluded phones or falsely flag objects. Microsleep, internal emotions, and true alertness are NOT reliably assessed by this prototype. If tracking is lost during a simulated drive, the virtual ignition is never switched off automatically.
