Visual perception for complex industrial environments

When workers and moving equipment share the same space, engineers need to evaluate how camera-based perception can complement their existing monitoring solutions. DIDBAN is an experimental platform to investigate these possibilities—not a certified safety product.

What DIDBAN demonstrates

Pedestrian Detection

Highlight people in camera images and explore visual monitoring zones.

Driver Attention Cues

Study eye closure, head turns, gaze estimates and phone visibility using a driver-facing camera.

Mobile Robot Awareness

Investigate detection output as a possible additional perception input in robot research.

Browser-Based Dashboard

Visualize monitoring outputs and selected session information in an experimental web interface.
